Linux understanding

made the switch to Linux full-time in 2026. I went with the Arch-based distro CachyOS because of the level of control and customisation it offers. I wanted to make the switch because I had already gained over two years of familiarity with Linux in my previous role, where Ubuntu was used . from that role I gained a good level of experience with CI/CD and Linux.

I like the idea that if I want something changed, I should be able to change it myself. With Windows, I felt that this was becoming harder and harder.

I wish I could say that I am good at ricing, but I ended up installing a dot environment that provided most of the desktop interface for me. Even so, I made sure to understand the keybinds and the features it offers, while still being able to go into the config files and change the keybinds and settings that I didn't like, such as two-finger right-click and the US keyboard layout.
